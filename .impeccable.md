## Design Context

### Users
This artifact is for students, founders, and operators hearing Aram tell a founder story in a talk/presentation context. They are scanning quickly on a projected screen or laptop and need a clear emotional arc, not a LinkedIn profile in slides.

### Brand Personality
Blunt, founder-native, low-bullshit. Human, earned, slightly editorial. Calm confidence over startup hype. The page should feel authored, not templated.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light mode first. Editorial / YC-adjacent founder deck, not corporate keynote and not AI-slop landing page. Real photos of Aram are the primary visual anchors. Avoid stock startup imagery, logo walls, generic dark gradients, glassmorphism, and repetitive card soup.

### Design Principles
1. One strong idea per slide.
2. Real founder tension beats polished résumé language.
3. Use Aram's real photos first; internet imagery is secondary texture only.
4. Fix hierarchy and composition before adding more content.
5. The deck must read clearly in 3–5 seconds per slide on a projector.
