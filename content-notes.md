# Content notes — drier version

## Spine

This version should feel less like a presentation and more like a smart person thinking out loud without trying to sound impressive.

Order:
1. I had ambition
2. I confused intensity with strength
3. That broke at scale
4. The real problem was architecture
5. That is why I care about AI that remembers

## Speaker notes by slide

### 1. Title
"I used to think intensity was enough. A lot of young founders think that. It helps. It is not a system."

### 2. About me
"The interesting part was not that I worked hard. Lots of people work hard. The interesting part was watching where work disappeared."

### 3. How I'm wired
"I am mostly interested in hidden structure. Why some people compound. Why some teams stay noisy. Why some work gets easier with time and some does not."

### 4. What shaped me
"Games teach you to look one layer deeper. That turned out to be useful everywhere."

### 5. First founder mode
"My first mode was simple: push harder. That works until the company gets larger than your memory."

### 6. Turning point
"I was not underpowered. I was badly organized. Those are different problems, and they have different fixes."

### 7. What changed
"Once you see it as an architecture problem, a lot of fake productivity stops looking impressive."

### 8. How I build now
"I want a way of working that still works when I am tired, distracted, or discouraged. Otherwise it is not real."

### 9. Where I'm going
"Most AI is still shallow. It helps once, then forgets everything. I think the interesting version remembers."

### 10. What is a startup
"A startup is a search. That is why it feels confusing from the inside."

### 11. Two ways to build
"One path is not more noble than the other. The problem starts when you pretend you want stability while secretly optimizing for upside."

### 12. Questions
"Most people do not need more inspiration. They need to answer one uncomfortable question honestly."

## Lines worth stealing

- "Intensity helps. It is not a system."
- "The company gets larger than your memory faster than you think."
- "A lot of fake productivity is just fear with better branding."
- "Most people do not need more advice. They need a more honest answer."
- "The interesting part of AI is not generation. It is memory."
- "Ambition gets you into the game. Structure is what lets you stay there."
