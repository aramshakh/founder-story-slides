Fast

Ideas came quickly. I could push hard under pressure.

Intense

Fragile

05 · Turning point

The bottleneck stopped being effort. It became architecture.

What broke

  • Too many threads in my head at once
  • Fragmented tools, fragmented context
  • Execution quality tied to energy level

Real insight

It’s easy for a founder to become the system instead of building one.

10 · Two ways to build

Normal business vs venture startup.

Normal business

  • Proven model, known demand
  • Profitable earlier, grows step by step
  • Usually funded by revenue or debt
  • Lower risk, more control

Venture startup

  • New, unproven, scalable idea
  • Built for speed and outsized growth
  • Often funded by investors
  • Higher risk, higher upside

Different games. Know which one you’re playing.
